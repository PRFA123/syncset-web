# Logo Vectorization Brief — SyncSet

**Project type:** Redraw an existing AI-generated logo concept as clean, production-ready vector art.
**Budget guidance:** ~US$30–60 for a skilled vector/logo specialist (not a beginner "trace my PNG" gig).
**Turnaround:** ask for 2–4 days with one revision round included.

---

## 1. What SyncSet is

SyncSet is an AI automation agency (workflow automation, CRM integration, AI agents) serving businesses of all sizes across Australia, expanding internationally. Brand attributes: **reliability, human warmth, quiet competence.** The mark should feel precise and trustworthy — not flashy, not "AI sci-fi," not a generic tech gradient logo.

## 2. The concept (keep this, change the execution)

Attached: two AI-generated concept images (`01_Gemini...jpg` and `02_Gemini...jpg`). **Attach both to this brief when you post it.**

The concept is an abstract letterform reading as **"S"** — two interlocking ribbon/loop shapes forming a twist, like an infinity symbol bent into an S. This concept is approved and should be preserved. **What is not approved is the execution style** — the reference images are AI-generated illustrations with organic, brush-like, feathered strokes (visible especially at the tapering tips). That painterly texture does not vectorize cleanly and will not survive at small sizes or in embroidery/screen-print. We need this **reinterpreted as flat, geometric vector art** — clean bezier curves, consistent stroke width, no feathering, no brush texture.

**Reference for terminal style:** compare the two attached images closely. Image 1 has **blunt, cut-off terminals** at the tips of the ribbon. Image 2 has **pointed, tapered terminals**. **Use Image 1's terminal style as the target** — the blunt cut reads better at small sizes and reproduces more reliably in print and embroidery. Image 2 is for mood/reference only, not to be replicated.

## 3. Deliverables (all as clean SVG, transparent background)

1. **Isolated mark** — the S-symbol alone, no wordmark. This is the primary asset; everything else derives from it.
2. **Horizontal lockup** — mark + wordmark "SyncSet" side by side.
3. **Stacked lockup** — mark above, wordmark below, centered.
4. **Monochrome version** — single solid color (for use as pure white on dark backgrounds, and pure black on light backgrounds). No gradients, no two-tone — the whole mark in one flat color.

**Do not draw the wordmark text as vector letterforms from the reference image.** The wordmark typeface is already decided (Plus Jakarta Sans, Medium weight, −1.5% tracking) — just place the correct text in that typeface. If you don't have access to Plus Jakarta Sans, leave a placeholder and note it; we'll swap the font ourselves.

## 4. Hard technical requirements

- **Format:** SVG (vector, editable paths — not a traced/embedded raster). Also provide a high-res PNG export of each deliverable for quick preview.
- **Background:** transparent. No paper texture, no shadow, no mockup framing — those were only in the reference images to sell the concept, not part of the actual asset.
- **Color:** solid, flat fill. Primary color for the mark on light backgrounds is near-black (`#101112`), not pure `#000000` — use that hex value.
- **Small-size survival:** the mark must remain legible and recognizable as the S-shape at **16×16px** (favicon size). If any detail disappears or turns into a blob at that size, simplify further — flag it to us rather than guessing.
- **Clear space & minimum size:** briefly note (even just a sentence) what minimum clear space around the mark you'd recommend, and the smallest size you'd consider safe for print/embroidery use.
- **No boolean-operation artifacts:** the interior negative space (the gap between the two interlocking loops) must read cleanly as intentional counters, not as leftover cut lines from a subtract operation.

## 5. What we already tried, so you don't repeat it

We attempted both a manual freehand trace of the reference image and a procedural geometric reconstruction (boolean circle/arc operations). Both failed to produce a clean, legible S — the freehand trace became an unreadable blob without interior counters, and the procedural version produced two disconnected arcs instead of an interlocking shape. **This is genuinely hand-crafted vector work, not a mechanical trace** — please treat the reference images as mood/concept boards, not literal templates to trace point-by-point.

## 6. Delivery

Send editable SVG source files (not flattened/outlined-only if avoidable) plus PNG previews at 512px and 64px so we can eyeball the small-size test ourselves. If you use Figma, Illustrator, or Affinity Designer, any of those source files are welcome alongside the SVG export.
