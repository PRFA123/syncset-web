# Market pricing — Northern Rivers and Australia, 30 Aug 2026

Desk research. Every figure below is a published claim from a vendor or
comparison site, in AUD, not a verified transaction. Vendor blogs have
an obvious interest in where they place the bands. Treat as orientation,
not as fact.

## What the customer's alternative costs

This is the anchor that matters — what a business owner already pays, or
would pay, to solve the same problem without us.

## Northern Rivers specifically

Agencies found serving Ballina, Byron, Lennox Head and Lismore: NRD
Marketing, Whitehat Agency, Gravitate Digital, Panorama Marketing (the
tourism specialist), Media Heroes, 4DP.

**None of them publish a price.** Every one is quote-on-request. One
comparison source notes that "almost every AI automation agency in
Australia quotes on request, which makes budgeting guesswork".

They are also all marketing agencies — SEO, Google Ads, paid social, web
design. None found positions on missed enquiry recovery or booking.

## What this means for our pricing

1. **A$350 is at the market median, not above it.** The typical published
   figure for a single automation is ~$349. The price is defensible.

2. **But we are not priced against agencies — we are priced against the
   customer's alternative.** For a solo tradie that alternative is an AI
   receptionist at $99–$149. Wayne's "fifty, hundred bucks" is not
   naive; it is roughly the market rate for the basic tier. He was
   answering accurately, not lowballing.

3. **For Marco's bracket the maths inverts.** His alternative was a
   person at ~$1,200/month, and a full Australian answering service is
   $1,000–$2,500. Against that, A$350 is cheap and easy to argue.

4. **The paid-anchor axis in NORTH_STAR is confirmed by market data,
   not just by n=3.** Where a business already pays for the function,
   the reference price is four to seven times ours. Where it does not,
   the reference price is a third to a half of ours.

5. **Price opacity is the local norm, so publishing a price is a
   differentiator.** Six agencies in the region, none showing a number.
   SMB owners dislike quote-on-request; Marco said it himself about
   restaurant CRMs — "price, mostly" was what put him off.

## Gap in this research
The established-agency half of the question is only partly answered.
Firms that publish prices skew newer and more self-serve; the mature
agencies quote privately, so their real numbers are not in this data.
To close that gap properly, ring three of the local six as a prospect
and ask. That is a phone call, not a search.
