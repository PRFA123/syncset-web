Verbatim. Their words, not summaries.

## 1. Wayne — solo plumber, Lismore / Ballina

Opening: accepted immediately. "Yeah nah, go for it mate, I'm just having
a smoko."

**How work arrives**
- "Phone mostly. Some Facebook messages, and the missus set up one of
  those Google Business things."
- "Phone, no question. That's where the real jobs come from."
- "Fifteen, twenty maybe. More after it rains hard."
- "Mornings are bad 'cause I'm usually under someone's house by then."
- "Storm season, mate. November through Feb it's mental."

**The core question** (five seconds of silence first)
- "...Honestly? It just rings out. I'm not gonna answer covered in shit
  under someone's house."
- "Phone was in the ute. Missed two calls in like an hour."
- "One picked up, said they'd already got someone."
- "Bloke sounded annoyed too, like I'd left him hanging."
- "He just texted 'all sorted thanks' — so, yeah, gone."
- **"Shits me honestly. It's not like I'm ignoring 'em on purpose."**
- "Probably a few times a week if I'm honest."

**Where it falls over:** FIRST CONTACT
- "If I don't get back to 'em quick they've rung the next bloke on Google."
- "Getting back to people fast enough. That's the whole problem."
- "Answering the bloody phone. Or at least not losing the ones I miss."

**Cost:** avg job $450-500 · 6-8 lost last month · worst $1200 hot water
system · "Nah I don't find out, that's the thing. They just go quiet."

**Already tried:** nothing. "Thought about an answering service once,
sounded expensive." Pays $0 today. Rejected GoHighLevel: "Looked
complicated as hell... I just want the phone thing sorted, not a whole
system."

**Worth:** "That's like a grand back in me pocket. I'd pay... fifty,
hundred bucks a month for that easy, if it actually worked."

**Names:** would call it "Never Miss a Call or somethin' obvious".
Nabbed → "like nabbing crooks? Dunno if that fits plumbing mate, haha."
Nothing Missed → "yeah, that one actually makes sense."

**Referral:** Dazza, sparky down the road. Pilot: "Yeah alright, come
show us."

## 2. Sarah — remedial massage therapist, Byron Bay

Opening: accepted with a guard. "As long as you're not selling me
anything, I get enough of that."

**How work arrives**
- "Instagram DMs mostly, and a booking link on my bio."
- "Maybe ten? It's pretty quiet some weeks."
- "Evenings, weirdly — people scrolling after work I guess."

**The core question**
- "I mean... I just reply when I get to it? I'm usually with a client so
  my phone's off."
- "Didn't see it till like 9pm." / "She'd already booked somewhere else."
- "She just said 'oh no worries I found someone' — polite about it."
- **"Honestly it's fine, it happens. I don't stress about it too much."**
- "Maybe once a fortnight?"

**Where it falls over:** says first contact, but names scheduling
- "People want a quick answer and I'm just... not always available."
- "Can take a day or two of back and forth honestly."
- "Probably the scheduling back-and-forth. I'd love to just not have to
  do that dance."

**Cost:** $120-160 a session · 2-3 a month · no big loss · "No, they just
disappear. I don't chase it up, feels weird to."

**Already tried:** Fresha for the calendar, free tier, works fine.
Rejected a chatbot: "it felt too... impersonal? People want to feel like
they're talking to me."
**"I don't want a robot talking to my clients about their back pain."**

**Worth:** "$250-300. I guess I'd pay something small for that, but
honestly I don't know if I'd trust it not to sound weird."

**Names:** would call it "Booked In". Nabbed → "doesn't really feel like
me, a bit aggressive for wellness." Nothing Missed → "fine I guess,
doesn't excite me either way." No face change on either.

**Referral:** friend who does sound baths. Pilot: "Yeah maybe — no
promises though, depends how it feels."

## 3. Marco — café owner + catering, Bangalow

Opening: sceptical. "I've got about ten minutes before the lunch rush."

**How work arrives**
- "Catering enquiries come through phone, email, and Instagram."
- "Phone, for the catering side. That's the real money."
- "Five or six a week."
- "Wedding season, spring — that's when catering enquiries go nuts."

**The core question** (long pause)
- "...It goes to voicemail. Nobody checks voicemail anymore though,
  let's be real."
- "Called back, left a message this time actually, then texted too."
- "Never heard back at all. Just silence."
- "I don't even know if I lost it or they just went quiet."
- **"Frustrates me because it might've been a $2000 event and I'll never
  know."**

**Where it falls over:** THE QUOTE, not first contact
- "Not really at first contact actually — most people do get through to
  someone eventually. It falls apart at the quote."
- "It sits in my inbox for two days because I'm flat out on the floor."
- "Should take a day. Realistically takes three, four."
- **"Me actually sitting down to write the quote. That's the bottleneck,
  not the phone."**
- "Honestly? The quoting. I'd hand that off in a heartbeat."

**Cost:** $800-2500 a job · 3-4 a month slow-quoted or lost · worst: a
40-person birthday, $1800, "quoted late, they went with the place next
door."

**Already tried:** hired a part-time person at $30/hr, ~10 hrs a week.
"It helped when she was rostered on, but she wasn't always around."
Couldn't justify the wage. Rejected restaurant CRMs: "way too much for
what I need... I just need the quoting bit sorted."

**Worth:** "That's easily two, three grand. I'd pay proper money for
that — like real money, not fifty bucks."

**Names:** would call it "Quote Fast". Nabbed → "sounds a bit like a
parking fine app, not gonna lie." Nothing Missed → face changed, "yeah
actually, that's closer, especially with the quote thing too."

**Referral:** Big Pete, marquees. Pilot: "Definitely, especially if it
actually handles the quoting side, not just the phone."
