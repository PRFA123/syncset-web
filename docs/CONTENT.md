- **Step 3 - Build:** We connect your existing tools and build the automation. Most first builds take 1–3 weeks. You own everything we build — the workflows, the data, the integrations. Not us.
- **Step 4 - Operate:** We monitor and adjust. If something breaks, we fix it — not you. No lock-in. If you ever want to take it in-house or switch providers, the system goes with you.
